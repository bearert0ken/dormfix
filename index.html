Questions

Do I pay before the repair?

Only for parts. If your repair needs a part, you pay for it before I order it, so I'm not stuck with a part nobody picks up. Labor is paid when you get your device back. Repairs with no part, like cleanings and software fixes, are paid at pickup. Mail-in repairs are paid in full before I ship them back.

Is diagnosis really free?

Yes. The only exception: if I have to open up a console or laptop to find the problem and you decide not to fix it, it's $10 for my time. Phones are always free to check.

What if it can't be fixed?

Then you don't pay anything. I'll tell you what's wrong and give it back.

How long does it take?

Most repairs take 1 to 2 days, depending on whether I need to order a part. Same-day rush is +$15 when I have the part on hand.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. If my repair or the part I installed fails within 30 days, I fix it free. It doesn't cover new drops, cracks, or water damage.

Will I lose my data?

Screen, battery, port, and cleaning repairs don't touch your data. A passcode reset erases the phone, so anything not backed up to iCloud or Google is gone. I'll always tell you before doing anything that wipes data.

Can you unlock a phone that isn't mine?

No. Passcode resets are only for your own device, and you'll need to sign back in with your Apple ID or Google account afterward. Activation Lock can't be removed without that login.

What parts do you use?

Affordable aftermarket parts, which is how prices stay this low. On iPhones, a non-Apple screen or battery shows an "Unknown Part" notice in Settings, and True Tone may not work after a screen swap. Want a higher-grade part? Ask and I'll quote it.

Will opening my console void the warranty?

It can. If your console is still under the manufacturer's warranty, check that first. I'll ask before opening anything.

Can I mail my device in?

Yes. Pick "Mail it in" on the form and I'll text you the shipping address. You cover shipping both ways, and return shipping gets added to your quote. Pack it well and back it up first.

What payment do you take?

Cash, Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle.
