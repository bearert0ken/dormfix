Questions

Before you book

Do I pay before the repair?

No. Diagnosis is free, you approve the price first, and you pay when you pick it up.

Will I lose my data?

Screen, battery, port, and cleaning repairs don't touch your data. A passcode reset erases the phone, so anything not backed up to iCloud or Google is lost. I'll always tell you before doing anything that wipes data.

Can you unlock a phone that isn't mine?

No. Passcode resets are only for your own device, and you'll need to sign back in with your Apple ID or Google account afterward. Activation Lock can't be removed without that login.

Will opening my console void the warranty?

It can. If your console is still under manufacturer warranty, check that option first. I'll ask before opening anything.

What payment do you take?

Cash, Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle at pickup.
